PortAtlas 4.0.2 makes each scan of `audit` cheaper on Linux. Commands, configuration files, logs and outputs behave as in 4.0.1.

## Improvements

- **Faster scans on Linux**: `audit` no longer reads the descriptors of every process at each scan to find which process holds each socket. It keeps what it read, checks each known owner again, and reads only the processes that started since the previous scan. A reused descriptor number or PID never leaves a socket on the wrong process. On a Linux machine with 41 processes holding 411 sockets, a scan went from about 50 ms to about 19 ms; most of what remains is the kernel producing the socket tables.

## Fixes

- **Sockets without a process on Linux**: when a process closed a descriptor while PortAtlas was reading its descriptor list, the other sockets of that process could be reported without their process for that scan. They now keep their process.

## Frameworks

SocketForge 2.1.1 (was 2.1.0). ProcessForge 1.3.0, FileForge.Core 1.4.1 and TopologyForge 1.13.0 are unchanged.
